# v0.33.0 — Design research summary

**Date:** 2026-08-17
**Author:** Mavis (research subagent; design synthesis by lead session)
**Purpose:** Background for `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-17-v0.33.0-needs-first-redesign.md`.

The research is organised by the five questions in the explore prompt. The
"Top 5" shippable moves at the end are what v0.33.0 actually ships.

---

## 1. Award-winning mental health apps — what the Apple Design Award tier is doing that MindAnchor isn't

1.1 **"Take a Breath" first frame.** Calm's first surface is a "Take a Deep
Breath" hero card before any content unlocks; the home shows progress and
recommendations only after that breath (Corporate Insight, 2024). Apple
Design Award 2022 *How We Feel* does the same — Mood Meter is the first
frame. Ship: a single breathing/cardiac-coherence tile on first unlock
per session, the rest fades in after.

1.2 **Illustration as brand, not decoration.** How We Feel uses geometric
shape morphology (sharp/jagged = stormy, soft/curved = serene) so the
visual language itself carries the affect (Sara Strand, 2024).
MindAnchor has no illustration system yet. Ship: a 24-shape vocabulary
where each shape is bound to a robust-z band, so the shape *is* the band.

1.3 **Single-action "Today" home, not a feed.** Headspace's Today tab
surfaces *one* recommended session, time-of-day-aware, and the user
research cited by Apple says they "stripped away complexity to focus on
the Today tab" (Apple Developer, "Behind the Design: Headspace").
Ship: formalise the home as "Today"; reveal the next card only after
the current one is engaged.

1.4 **Sleep needs different design rules than Day.** Headspace's sleep
UI is dark, low-contrast, large touch targets for half-closed eyes
(Crosley). Ship: 22:00–06:00 window → drop contrast, raise targets to
56dp, soften the time serif.

1.5 **HWF's passive HealthKit cross-correlation.** HWF pulls sleep +
exercise via HealthKit to surface emotional trends ("on days with 8h
sleep, yellow-hued emotions") — passive, not active logging (Apple App
Store Story, 2022). Ship: one line on the home with one
cross-correlation between HRV/RHR and sleep (n-of-1 framing).

## 2. BPD-specific UX research (2024–2026)

2.1 **Lindsay et al. 2024, JMIR meta-analysis (n=42 studies).**
Digital interventions significantly reduce *suicidal ideation* (g = –0.13)
and *paranoia* (g = –0.52) but NOT overall BPD severity (g = –0.17,
n.s.). Three design features predicted bigger effects: **reminders,
self-monitoring, encouraging users to rehearse behaviours.** Ship:
a rehearsal affordance ("this is what I do when X"), keep self-
monitoring visible but never auto-grade it, reminders opt-in only.

2.2 **Priovi RCT, Lancet Psychiatry 2025; 12:366–376.** First
commercial schema-therapy digital therapeutic to show effect on BPD
(d = 0.24) AND a 3× lower suicide attempt rate (IRR 0.34, p = 0.008).
Critical design detail: **unguided, fully self-paced, safety-plan-on-
home.** Ship: a one-tap "safety plan" tile on the launcher.

2.3 **DBT Coach feasibility (Rizvi et al., APA PsycNet).** App's
value is in the acute moment, not cumulative. Median use was 11.5
times over 9 months. Ship: every card should answer "what does this
card do for me in the next 5 minutes?"

2.4 **Wise Mind / DEAR MAN / GIVE / FAST missing from v0.27+.** Ship:
Wise Mind as a card on the home, not buried in a skill tree.

2.5 **BPD-specific safety rule: no urgency colours.** Sam Goldstein's
clinical UX review (gapsystudio.com/blog/mental-health-app-design) is
explicit: "strictly limit the use of high-vibrancy reds or oranges."
MindAnchor's palette already follows this — codify it in a token
called `urgency.banned` and lint the design system.

## 3. Health data dashboard patterns — showing 3 sources

3.1 **Apple Health's "preferred source" model.** When Garmin, Oura,
Whoop all push HRV, HealthKit picks one as the trend driver; lower-
priority sources still write but don't drive charts (apptizo.com
2026). Ship: a `metric_owner` table. For each metric, pick ONE primary
source. Show a one-character "source glyph" (⌚Oura / ⌚Garmin /
📷camera-PPG) on the home card.

3.2 **Audit-trail pattern.** WearSync normalises per-source timestamps
and a source label on every datapoint (Reddit r/Garmin, 2025). Ship:
every datapoint has `source_id`, `captured_at`, `ingested_at`; surface
"last sync 2h ago" as the only freshness cue.

3.3 **Conflicting sources — show the disagreement.** Apple Health does
not resolve conflicting sleep numbers; it shows the spread (e.g.,
"6.2–7.4h across sources"). MindAnchor's n-of-1 framing actually fits
this — own-range-from-own-sources, never a single authoritative number.

3.4 **Trend window = 7-day rolling, not 30.** "Two weeks of consistent
tracking reveals more than two months of sporadic logging" (kygo.app
2025). MindAnchor's 180-day prune + 14-day floor are aligned. Ship:
default trend window on the HRV card to "last 7 days" with "last 30"
behind one tap.

3.5 **Source priority default (Oura/Coros convention).** Verified May
2026 (apptizo.com): HRV → Oura first, Whoop second. Steps → Garmin.
Sleep → Oura only. MindAnchor's Coros Pace 3 is the Garmin-equivalent
wrist device; camera PPG is the manual fallback. Ship: default Coros
for steps/RHR/active energy, camera-PPG only when no watch is present.

## 4. Material 3 / Compose patterns (2025–2026)

4.1 **M3 Expressive "hero moment".** Stick to **one or two hero
moments** in the whole product (m3.material.io/blog/building-with-m3-
expressive). Ship: the "I need help right now" tile is MindAnchor's
hero moment. Reserve all M3 Expressive tactics for that tile.

4.2 **Spring physics as default motion.** M3 Expressive makes
`rememberSpringSpec()` the default. Ship: replace the launcher's
existing ease-in-out curves with
`spring(stiffness = 380f, dampingRatio = Spring.DampingRatioNoBouncy)`.

4.3 **FAB Menu / Split Button.** M3's FAB Menu opens from a FAB to
display multiple related actions; M3 Expressive added Split Button.
Ship: use a split-button for "I need to do something" — primary tap
goes to the most-likely DBT skill, chevron opens TIPP / STOP / Pros &
Cons.

4.4 **Calm, balanced layouts (Aura example).** Google's reference
breathing app, Aura, uses "a large central flower" — pause/stop less
prominent than the focal element. Ship: the breathing tile's
animation is the visually dominant element; controls are smaller.

4.5 **Reduce motion, AND non-motion fallback for dissociative users.**
Even when motion is enabled, **never animate anything that auto-
loops** — a breathing circle that auto-loops can entrap a dissociative
user (cross-ref with Linehan 1993 distress tolerance).

## 5. "What do you need right now?" home-card patterns

5.1 **Wysa / Calm / HWF 3-zone pattern.** Both surface the mood
check-in as the gateway to a mood-personalised toolset (Corporate
Insight 2024). Ship: a 3-card row labelled with first-person
validate-then-suggest phrasing (NOT "I need to..." — DBT framing is
*acknowledge first, then choose*). Three cards: "Hear me out" /
"Help me do something" / "Just check in".

5.2 **Crisis button is a separate persistent element.** Gapsystudio
guide (2024): "a persistent, high-contrast button that remains
accessible regardless of which screen the user is on… persistent
navigation is treated as a safety requirement." Ship: the crisis card
must *not* live inside the 3-card row. It is a dedicated, fixed-
position affordance.

5.3 **State-aware home.** "Single-Path Interface" — state-aware
screens that offer one clear next step based on the user's current
mood or energy level (gapsystudio). Ship: when any BPD-safety flag
fires (recent dissociation, sleep-missed, elevated RHR), the 3-card
row collapses to *one* card — the validate-first card, with the
other two still reachable in 1 tap.

5.4 **No "I need" framing.** Wysa's chatbot opens with "Take a Deep
Breath" *before* asking any question. Reflectly's first action is a
mood slider with *no question text*. Ship: replace any "I need to..."
labelling with a 2-word verb phrase, or no text at all (icon-only on
the inner card, label reveals on long-press).

5.5 **14-day floor + direction bands (already shipped) align with the
Wysa/EU evidence base.** Ship: add depth to the cards MindAnchor
already has, not more cards.

## Top 5 shippable moves for v0.33.0

1. **"What do you need right now?" 3-card row** (Wysa/HWF pattern,
validate-then-suggest, NOT "I need" — DBT framing). State-aware
collapse to 1 card when any BPD-safety flag fires. *Replaces* the
current "How is it right now? / Ground me here" card.

2. **"Where it comes from" data sources card** (Apple Health preferred-
source model, 3 sources, source glyph, audit-trail timestamp). Makes
the existing Health Connect + Coros + PPG integration visible for
the first time.

3. **Rehearsal tile + safety plan tile as two separate home cards**
(Lindsay 2024 + Priovi 2025). The rehearsal tile is a 3-step
"trigger → skill → outcome". The safety plan tile is a 1-tap
reach to the user-edited crisis contacts already required by the
BPD-safe default. *(v0.34.0 — research says these need to exist, but
they don't have to ship in v0.33.0. The ship list is moves 1, 2, 4, 5.)*

4. **M3 Expressive "hero moment" reserved for the validate-first tile,
spring physics everywhere else** (`rememberSpringSpec()`, FAB split-
button for "do something" branch, shape morph ONLY on the hero).

5. **Crisis affordance as a separate, fixed-position element** (top-
left, never moves across versions) — preserved from the existing
home as the "Open Support" button.

---

## What v0.33.0 actually ships (decision)

Top-5 moves 1, 2, 4, 5. Move 3 (rehearsal + safety plan tiles) is the
v0.34.0 follow-up — the v0.33.0 home cannot take four new tiles
without losing the "validate first" affordance, and adding depth to
existing cards is the v0.33.0 spec's explicit design move (per
finding 5.5: "add depth to the cards MindAnchor already has, not
more cards").

The crisis tile (move 5) is already the top-left "Open Support"
button on the home and the Support screen's reach-someone card. v0.33.0
preserves it; no change needed.

M3 Expressive (move 4) ships partially in v0.33.0 — spring physics is
added to the validate-first tile, FAB split-button is deferred to
v0.34.0 (no FAB currently exists on the home).

**Net v0.33.0 ship list:** "What do you need right now?" card (replaces
distress card) + "Where it comes from" data sources card (new) +
spring physics on the validate-first tile + existing crisis
affordance preserved.
