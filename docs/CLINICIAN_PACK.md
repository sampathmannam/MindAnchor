## What this app promises about wording

One rule shapes every sentence: research in the app says what a
signal *is*, the person's own history says it *moved*, and nothing
anywhere says what that *means about them*. No diagnosis, no risk
language, no praise or blame, no streaks, no badges. Empty states
are treated as ordinary. If a line below breaks that rule, it is a
bug of exactly the kind this review exists to catch.

## How to review

Read each line as three people: someone having an ordinary day,
someone in a depressive episode who is keeping score against
themselves, and someone recently bereaved whose sleep and numbers
are legitimately terrible. Flag anything that reads as reproach,
alarm, cheerfulness at the wrong moment, or certainty the data
cannot carry.
