## What this app promises about wording

One rule shapes every sentence: research in the app says what a
signal *is*, the person's own history says it *moved*, and nothing
anywhere says what that *means about them*. No diagnosis, no risk
language, no praise or blame, no streaks, no badges. Empty states
are treated as ordinary. If a line below breaks that rule, it is a
bug of exactly the kind this review exists to catch.

## How to review

Read each line as three people: someone having an ordinary day,
someone in a depressive episode who is keeping score against
themselves, and someone recently bereaved whose sleep and numbers
are legitimately terrible. Flag anything that reads as reproach,
alarm, cheerfulness at the wrong moment, or certainty the data
cannot carry.

- **`skill_stop_title`** — STOP
- **`skill_stop_body`** — Stop. Take a step back. Observe what is happening, inside and around you. Proceed mindfully — pick the next small action, not the whole day.
- **`skill_tipp_title`** — TIPP
- **`skill_tipp_body`** — Cold water on your face or hands. Intense movement for a minute. Paced breathing, out longer than in. Then relax each muscle group in turn. These change body chemistry fast when thinking alone will not.
- **`skill_tipp_caution`** — The cold water and the hard movement both swing your heart rate on purpose. If you have a heart condition, an eating disorder, or are pregnant, skip those two and ask a doctor first. The paced breathing and the muscle relaxation are gentle, and they work on their own.

- **`skill_grounding_body`** — Name five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell, one you can taste. Slowly, out loud if you can.
