A phone that succeeds
when you do, not
when you don't put it down.

MindAnchor is a launcher and notification layer that inverts the usual objective. It doesn't optimize for engagement or screen time. It succeeds when your attention, sleep and mood measurably improve. Every feature it ships cites the published study behind it.

What it does

Six features, each tied to a mechanism in the research, not a guess about what feels good.

01 Batched notifications

Machine notifications collapse into a few calm daily digests. The humans you choose still come through instantly.

Fitz et al. 2019: batching improved mood and attention; muting everything backfired.

02 Text-first launcher

Search to open. A handful of favorites. No grid, no badges, no red dots to pull your eye.

03 Self-chosen friction

A breathing-paced pause, an intention prompt, and time-boxed sessions on the apps that pull at you, opt-in per app.

04 Sunset mode

From your own wind-down hour: grayscale, feeds gated, everything batched until morning.

05 Sleep-regularity tracking

Inferred from screen on/off patterns alone: no wearable, no cloud round-trip required.

06 Honest measurement

Short wellbeing pulses. If a feature isn't helping you specifically, the app says so and offers to turn itself off.

Principles

The constraints that shape every decision in the codebase.

  • —
    Zero backend. No accounts, no server, no analytics. Your data stays on your phone, full stop.
  • —
    Evidence or it doesn't ship. Every intervention cites the study it implements. Features argued on vibes alone get declined.
  • —
    Autonomy, not paternalism. Everything is a toggle. Nothing is imposed on you by default.
  • —
    Wellness, not medicine. MindAnchor is not a medical device and does not diagnose. See the crisis resources below if you need a human right now.
