MindAnchor is an open-source, mental-health-first launcher and notification layer.
Every feature implements a published research finding:

* Machine notifications are batched into a few calm daily digests, while the people
  you choose always reach you instantly.
* A text-first, badge-free home screen replaces the app grid.
* Apps you flag get a breathing-paced pause and intention prompt before opening,
  with time-boxed sessions.
* Sunset mode protects your wind-down hour: grayscale, feeds gated, notifications
  held until morning.
* Sleep regularity is estimated from screen on/off patterns — no wearable needed.
* Short wellbeing check-ins tell you honestly whether the features are helping you.

No accounts. No server. No analytics. Your data never leaves your phone.

MindAnchor is a wellness tool, not a medical device. If you are struggling, please
reach a human: 988 (US), Tele-MANAS 14416 (India), or your local crisis line.
